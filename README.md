### Lead Empennage Design Engineer

An ongoing SAE collegiate aircraft design project focused on structural optimization, CAD development, manufacturing, and airframe integration. My primary responsibility has been leading the design and development of the aircraft empennage while supporting overall structural architecture and vehicle integration efforts.

---

## Project Overview

As Lead Empennage Design Engineer, I was responsible for the design, modeling, and integration of the aircraft's tail assembly, including the vertical stabilizer, rudder, and supporting structure. The project required balancing structural strength, manufacturability, and weight reduction while ensuring compatibility with the aircraft's overall configuration.

In addition to empennage development, I contributed heavily to the aircraft's internal structural architecture, helping develop truss configurations, skeletal structures, and spar integration methods used throughout the airframe.

## Key Contributions

- Led the design and development of the entire empennage assembly.
- Designed the aircraft's vertical stabilizer and rudder structures.
- Created and refined detailed aircraft CAD assemblies in Autodesk Inventor.
- Developed lightweight truss configurations to maximize structural efficiency while minimizing mass.
- Contributed to airframe skeletonization efforts to reduce overall aircraft weight.
- Supported structural architecture decisions regarding spar placement and integration through the fuselage.
- Assisted manufacturing and assembly efforts by ensuring components were practical to fabricate and integrate.
- Collaborated with multidisciplinary engineering teams throughout the design-build process.
